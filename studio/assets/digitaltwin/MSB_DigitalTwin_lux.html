The Problem

No breeding blanket, no fusion power plant

A DT reactor burns tritium that does not exist in nature. It must breed its own from lithium via 6Li(n,t)4He, with a tritium breeding ratio (TBR) above ~1.05 to stay self-sufficient — and the entire margin is only 5–15%. Detailed 3D neutronics tells you the TBR of one design, slowly. You cannot search thousands of designs that way.

Thin margin
Required TBR 1.05–1.15; achievable ceiling ~1.15 for the best 3D designs. Miss it and the plant starves (Abdou 2021, Sawan & Abdou 2006).
Slow to explore
Each Monte-Carlo point is minutes to hours. Sweeping enrichment × thickness × multiplier × geometry is out of reach interactively.
Trust must transfer
A black-box surrogate you cannot audit is unusable for a first-of-a-kind blanket you cannot yet measure.

Our answer: a glass-box reduced model that runs in microseconds, calibrated to OpenMC, wrapped into a digital twin that optimizes the blanket and states its own limits.

Anchors: Abdou et al 2021 (Nucl. Fusion 61 013001) · Sawan & Abdou 2006 (Fus. Eng. Des. 81 1131)

The Breeder

FLiBe — the molten-salt breeding blanket

A molten fluoride salt (2LiF·BeF₂, "FLiBe") is breeder, coolant, and neutron moderator in one. The ARC design immerses the whole vessel in it.

⁶Li + n → T + ⁴He  (+4.78 MeV, ~940 b thermal)
The workhorse: exothermic, huge thermal cross section. Enriching Li-6 above natural 7.59% raises breeding.
⁷Li + n → T + ⁴He + n'  (threshold ~2.8 MeV)
Fast channel: breeds AND conserves the neutron. Matters most for the 14.1 MeV DT source spectrum.
Be / Pb (n,2n)  neutron multiplier
A thin multiplier layer makes up the neutron deficit so TBR can clear 1.0 with margin.

The Methodology

AxiomFabric™ — four pillars, applied to breeding

Physics that doesn't change, times intelligence that learns from data, equals an auditable twin.

01 · PLANTFORGE
Reduced physics
A glass-box 1D two-group slab TBR model — the 6Li e-folding, effective path, multiplier factor, parasitic loss. Runs in microseconds.
02 · OPENMC
Ground truth

Calibrate + close the gap

Honest Limits

What this twin does not claim

Two honest bands
The raw 1D slab carries a ±30% model-form band vs 3D MC. After calibration to OpenMC the twin's residual is <1%, so the reported band is the ±10% OpenMC-vs-reality uncertainty (nuclear data + geometry) — never removed.
1D-spherical geometry
The OpenMC ground truth is a 1D sphere with homogenized steel and a coverage factor — realistic in magnitude (TBR ~1.0–1.1, near ARC's 1.08) but not a specific 3D machine CAD. Calibrated to this reference, not yet to a named device.
Nuclear-data sensitivity
TBR carries 5–15% modeling and nuclear-data uncertainty — comparable to the whole self-sufficiency margin. TBR has never been measured in an integrated system (ITER TBMs will give the first data).
Screening tier
This is a design-screening twin, not a licensing calculation. It rank-orders and narrows the space fast; final designs still need full 3D MC.

The point: a twin you can trust is one that shows its physics and states its band. That is the AxiomFabric contract.
